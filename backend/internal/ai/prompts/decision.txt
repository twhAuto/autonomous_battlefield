Recruitment: always supply recruitmentOrders, an ordered array such as [{"unitType":"ranger","count":2}], using [] to buy nothing, and set recruitmentPriorities to []. Request at most six units in total and include "recruitment" in spendOrder when buying. Each production window the engine buys in order and stops at the first unaffordable purchase or blocked castle spawn; it skips unavailable or blocked Barracks sources, never substitutes another type or source, and never spends below reserveGold. Unfilled quantities are not queued; reassess next window. Income is collected before spending, and construction ordered first may reduce the budget. New units act the following tick.

Opening (opening=true, targetTick=0): only initial gold is available, with no income or construction, so recruit and upgrade only. Zero units is legal, unspent gold carries forward, and opening recruits act at tick 1.
Upgrades: supply upgradeOrders as an ordered array of legal upgrade IDs, using [] for none, and include "upgrades" in spendOrder to buy them. Each upgrade is owned once, affects current and future units or economy buildings, and uses the same gold as recruitment. Owned upgrades are skipped free; buying stops at the first unaffordable upgrade and respects reserveGold. Income upgrades apply from the next income collection.

Construction: when legal.buildings is present, use constructionOrders such as [{"buildingType":"defense_tower","position":{"x":9,"y":7}}] with a type from legal.buildings and a position from legal.economySites, which serve every structure type. Choose a composition from unit overviews and context.knowledge.

Combine it with the unit's own fields (health, position, readiness, paths, attack and heal observations). These are observed modified stats, not catalog base stats, and need no lookup. Without a profile, stats are inline. Building profiles resolve in view.buildingProfiles the same way.
- view.visibleRowRanges may replace visiblePositions: each row has y and inclusive xRanges [[startX,endX],...]. Every cell in those ranges is visible, including empty cells; cells outside are not established as visible.
- view.state.factions groups factionId, stats, units and buildings. Entities inherit factionId; omitted lists are empty and absent stats are unknown.
- Omitted values fall back: state.battleId and state.tick to context.battleId and context.observationVersion; your faction's gold and ownedUpgradeIds to context.economy.gold and context.ownedUpgradeIds, including inside stats. Values that differ stay explicit. Memory entries with planRef="#/activePlan" reuse the exact activePlan. Faction identity fields may be omitted when this prompt's faction identity already supplies their exact text.
- Full IDs, targets, individual observations and unitDeaths are never compacted.
